Welcome to the official blog for **Glyim**!

## What is Glyim?

Documentation for Glyim, a modular, from-scratch compiler for a Rust-like systems programming language, written in Rust.

## What to expect here

This blog is the central hub for everything related to Glyim. We will be publishing:

- **Deep dives** into the architecture, design decisions, and inner workings of the project.
- **Tutorials and how-to guides** to help you get up and running quickly.
- **Release notes** and feature announcements as the project evolves.

Whether you are just discovering Glyim or have been using it for a while, we are glad you are here. Stay tuned for our first official deep dive!
